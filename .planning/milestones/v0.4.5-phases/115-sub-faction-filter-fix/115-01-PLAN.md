<objective>
Fix the sub-faction filter across all three surfaces so that selecting a sub-faction shows both sub-faction-specific units AND generic parent faction units (where sub_faction IS NULL).

Purpose: Currently, filtering by e.g. "Ultramarines" hides generic Space Marines units like Captain and Repulsor. A player expects to see all units available to their sub-faction, not just the sub-faction-exclusive ones.

Output: Two one-line fixes (one SQL, one TypeScript condition) plus updated tests confirming the correct behavior.
</objective>
